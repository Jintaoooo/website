01ABOUT ME · 关于我

Between data
and the Earth.在数据与
地球之间。

I study terrestrial weathering products — bauxite, laterite and paleosol — through geochemistry, machine learning and large geochemical datasets.我关注铝土矿、红土和古土壤等陆地风化产物，结合地球化学、机器学习与大规模地球化学数据开展研究。

My broader questions connect multi-sphere Earth processes with bauxite genesis, provenance and the metallogeny of rare earth elements.我的核心问题是：多圈层地球过程如何驱动铝土矿成因、物源变化与稀土元素成矿？
